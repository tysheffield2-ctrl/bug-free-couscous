# ENCORE 0.9.5 Beta 1

- Rebuilt Charts as a visual weekly leaderboard with a top-three presentation, recent chart-path graphics, movement treatment, ranking meters and weekly-volume bars while preserving ENCORE's existing chart math.
- Consolidated business navigation under Business HQ so offers, contracts, labels, representation, staff, creative work, merchandise, sports ownership and catalog holdings are entered from one command center instead of separate Menu destinations.
- Added a persistent Business HQ department bar while inside business screens so every department clearly belongs to the same business workspace.
- Added priority opportunity popups for new feature requests, collaborations, production and writing requests, song opportunities, tour invitations and selected commercial/career opportunities.
- Priority popups never auto-accept an opportunity; players can review it immediately or leave it for later in its normal screen.
- Added a Business HQ priority desk that keeps open creative and commercial requests visible after the popup is dismissed.
- Added regression smoke coverage for the 0.9.5 chart, Business HQ and notification layer.
