# ENCORE 0.9.1 beta — Touring 2.0 and city scenes

- Plan city-by-city routes with editable dates, venues, standard/premium tickets, production, security and real opening artists.
- Shows resolve weekly, up to two per week, using regional audience, local loyalty, scene heat, price resistance and repeat-visit fatigue.
- Review itemized crew, production, security, travel, lodging and opener costs before committing.
- Keep per-show attendance, satisfaction, incidents, regional fan growth and PULSE reach reports.
- Successful performances lift local demand and catalog streams; weak demand can lose money and unfunded shows pause.
- Receive and accept opening-slot invitations from established artists.
- Preserve legacy tour earnings and migrate remaining shows into an editable estimated route.
- Keep a permanent hometown while relocating your current city; artist geographic identities and city scenes persist.
- New unoccupied properties in a tour city reduce lodging costs. Historical properties without locations are not assigned invented cities.
- Save schema 2 upgrades foundation and older careers through ordered migrations.
