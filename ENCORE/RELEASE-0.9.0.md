# ENCORE 0.9.0 Beta 1 — Studio Overhaul Preview

- Begins the creative-song-process overhaul without replacing the stable 0.8.7 release.
- Adds creative direction choices for theme, tempo and artistic intent.
- Adds a six-part song identity: lyrical depth, production, performance, catchiness, originality and commercial appeal.
- Separates artistic strength from commercial potential so excellent songs are not automatically hits.
- Existing Writing, Recording, Producing, Charisma, Marketability and Viral Ability skills feed the new identity model.
- Recording and mixing decisions continue to alter the finished record and now refresh its creative identity.
- Adds a more immersive Studio visual treatment while keeping ENCORE a career simulator rather than a DAW.
- This work lives on the feature/v090-studio-overhaul branch until testing is complete.

## Foundation stabilization (beta.2)

- Route fan changes through one audience event entry point and conserve regional/global fan totals.
- Migrate untracked geography with estimated genre shares; preserve observed stream history.
- Show first tracked regional growth as NEW and keep Career Pulse on its own screen.
- Consolidate weekly advancement and explicit month-end hooks, with rollback protection.
- Add ordered career-state migrations and reject future state schemas.
- Require executive readiness for new normal-career labels; preserve existing label owners.
- Serve heavy artwork as separate offline-cached assets instead of embedding it in gameplay JavaScript.
- Add deterministic 1-, 5-, 10- and 20-year validation and foundation regressions.
- Damp long-career AI audience growth and add audience decay without changing player royalty rates.
- Fix numeric-input initialization in non-browser environments and clarify career overhead.

This remains a beta candidate. These changes do not represent completion of the full Road to 1.0 or verified public deployment.
