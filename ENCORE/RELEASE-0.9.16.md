# ENCORE 0.9.16-beta.1 — Living Industry / Competition 2.0

- The industry can now generate meaningful breakout shocks, sleeper surges, comeback records, major-label pushes and catalog revivals instead of relying only on steady background growth.
- Competitive surges change real NPC weekly and lifetime stream totals, so they can materially alter the same charts the player is competing on.
- Surprise #1 records are detected from the authoritative chart ledger, recorded as persistent industry events and surfaced through briefings instead of appearing as unexplained chart noise.
- Rival pressure now persists across weeks, tracking competitive overlap, rival heat, peak weekly activity and #1 control.
- World Pulse adds a dedicated Competition 2.0 panel with current industry pressure, release congestion, the active industry leader, closest rivals and recent shocks.
- NPC profiles can show their persistent competitive history when a meaningful rivalry with the player develops.
- Industry shocks use deterministic per-week/per-artist rolls so the same resolved week is stable rather than changing because a screen re-rendered.
- Major competition events also write into the Cohesion Core career-event direction, allowing later systems to remember major chart and rivalry moments.
- The system is bounded: one competition surge per artist per week, capped multipliers, finite event history and decaying inactive rival records.
- Cohesion Core Artist File legal-record injection was hardened so the permanent Legal / Business Record survives presentation variations instead of depending on one exact footer string.
