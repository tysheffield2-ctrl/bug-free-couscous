# ENCORE 0.9.2 Beta 1

- Rebuilt feature business around a player-set asking price with no career-imposed ceiling.
- Added cash, royalty and hybrid feature negotiations; artists can pass when your price exceeds your leverage or their budget.
- Added ongoing simulated royalty payouts for accepted feature splits.
- Reworked merchandise with demand forecasts, preorders, print-on-demand unlimited inventory, giveaways and unrestricted price entry.
- Made contract rights checkboxes update their meaning immediately and feed editable rights packages into roster interest.
- Removed the duplicate legacy creative-direction block when the newer 0.9 Studio creative brief is active.
- Expanded ENCORE Honors results with winning work names, simulated panel votes and career wins by category.
- Removed the 10% sports ownership cap; players can buy any available stake up to full franchise ownership.
- Added controlling-owner front-office decisions for roster investment, facilities, marketing and franchise strategy.
- Added Business HQ as a command center for collaborations, merchandise, deals, label operations, catalogs, sports and finances.
- Improved numeric readbacks so decimal values remain visible instead of being truncated.
