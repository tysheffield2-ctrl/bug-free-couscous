# ENCORE 0.9.2 Beta 1

- Rebuilt feature economics around artist-set asking prices, market demand, cash fees, royalty splits and hybrid deals.
- Added recurring royalty statements for feature appearances and backend royalty obligations for guests on player records.
- Replaced the duplicate song-start flow with one unified 0.9 creative brief and production interface.
- Reworked label negotiations into one live clause editor with responsive rights labels and a package-wide interest meter.
- Added merchandise demand estimates, inventory launches, preorders, unlimited on-demand fulfillment and giveaway campaigns.
- Expanded ENCORE Honors results with winning song/album/work, simulated panel votes and repeat-category career win counts.
- Removed the arbitrary 10% sports-stake ceiling; players can build to 100% ownership and invest across the full franchise market.
- Added controlling-owner sports decisions for competitive strategy, ticket strategy and capital/facilities investment.
- Added Business HQ with executive skills, business asset tracking and direct access to contracts, labels, merch, creative work, sports and catalogs.
- Fixed large-number readbacks so decimal prices stay readable instead of being truncated.
