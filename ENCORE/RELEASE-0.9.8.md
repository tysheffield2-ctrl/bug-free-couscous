# ENCORE 0.9.8

- Rebuilt Business HQ around a decision desk and four focused business lanes instead of one long department directory.
- Added a compact Business section menu so child screens no longer carry the entire horizontal department strip.
- Reworked Contracts & Rights into focused Overview, Agreements, Rights, and Catalog Intelligence views.
- Moved catalog valuation detail behind an explicit deep-dive tab so it no longer dominates every contract visit.
- Upgraded paid career opportunities such as interviews, opening slots, feature invitations, and festival sets to the same page-by-page legal agreement experience used for artist contracts.
- Added explicit rights/ownership language to one-time opportunity agreements so they do not imply transfer of masters, publishing, catalog, merchandise, touring, or unrelated endorsement rights.
- Added mobile-specific layout rules for the new Business HQ and Deal Room navigation.
