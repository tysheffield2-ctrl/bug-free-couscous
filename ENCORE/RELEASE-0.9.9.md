# ENCORE 0.9.9

- Added Tax Foundation 2.0 with a lower gameplay-first progressive federal tax curve from 0% to 32%.
- Regional taxes now add 0–4 percentage points with a 36% simulated marginal-rate ceiling.
- Weekly reports now show gross income, eligible deductions and taxable income separately before the tax reserve.
- Added a Tax Planning snapshot to the financial report with year-to-date gross income, deductions, estimated taxable income, liability and reserve.
- Preserved existing career-expense, contract, commission and operating-cost accounting as the authoritative deduction base.
- Clarified that taxable income is a calculation base, not a second cash deduction.
- Kept ENCORE tax rules fictionalized for gameplay rather than representing a real tax return.
