# ENCORE 0.9.7 Beta 1

- Gave persistent AI artists distinct career strategies: steady builder, prolific, perfectionist, connector and era builder.
- Career strategy now changes release cadence and execution instead of every artist following the same release clock.
- Connected the simulated genre climate to actual AI release outcomes; hot and cooling genres now affect performance without guaranteeing success.
- Added persistent AI collaboration history and a collaboration-focused career strategy that can create recurring creative networks.
- Connected new-generation artists to the existing label league, including label budgets and the existing transfer / renewal economy instead of creating a parallel label simulation.
- Added career-stage storytelling for breakout, prime, veteran, retirement and comeback transitions in World Pulse.
- Added retirement legacy summaries with peak audience, catalog streams, biggest release, chart peak, #1 weeks, career releases and career length.
- Retired artists now close their active AI label contract while preserving the former label and full career identity; comebacks return independent and can write a new label chapter later.
- Upgraded AI artist profiles with generation, strategy, status, label, peak audience, career age, collaborator count and preserved legacy record.
- Added regression coverage for strategy diversity, genre response, label integration, collaboration history, legacy persistence, comeback continuity and save-budget safety.

This pass continues Charts & AI Industry 2.0. AI touring, richer label executive strategy, scene networks and deeper award / legacy integration remain follow-up work.
