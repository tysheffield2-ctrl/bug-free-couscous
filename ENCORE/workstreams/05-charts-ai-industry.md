# ENCORE — Charts & AI Industry

## Audit score
**Charts: 6.2/10** · **AI Industry: 5.9/10**

## Current state
Charts correctly track floors, debut, movement, re-entry, peak, weeks, #1 streaks and late entries. NPCs have momentum, cooling, hiatus, reinvention, collaboration and comeback behavior. However, charts still use synthetic background competition and the active world is still largely built around the original roster rather than true generations.

## Canonical goal
A persistent music industry that exists independently of the player: artists emerge, develop, peak, slump, retire, return, leave legacies and are replaced by new generations. Chart entries should overwhelmingly belong to persistent careers.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Create new-artist generation and development pipelines.
- Add aging, career-stage behavior, retirement/inactivity and legacy persistence.
- Replace synthetic chart filler gradually with persistent artists/releases.
- Track durable artist/release identity behind every meaningful chart entry.
- Give AI artists strategy: release cadence, touring, collaborations, label moves and genre/scene response.
- Let genres/scenes trend independently of player success.
- Validate chart distribution, #1 thresholds, longevity and world audiences over 20+ years.
- Preserve history when an artist leaves the active roster.

## Why this is Tier A
This is the biggest remaining gap between ENCORE as a strong career game and ENCORE as a believable long-running music-world simulation.

## Do not drift
Do not solve chart realism by generating more anonymous rows. Every new generation should increase persistent world depth.
