# ENCORE — Song Creation / Studio 2.0

## Audit score
**8.1/10**

## Current state
The active Studio uses one unified creative start flow with theme, tempo, intent, texture, hook, mood, writing approach and production budget. Skills and choices create tradeoffs across lyrical depth, production, performance, catchiness, originality and commercial appeal. Projects persist and unfinished work carries forward.

## Canonical goal
Make creating a record feel like a sequence of artistic decisions rather than configuring inputs and receiving a score. Preserve a fast path for players who do not want every song to become a long minigame.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Deepen writing/demo/recording/production/mix/master into optional iterative stages.
- Let revision improve one dimension while risking/costing another.
- Create identifiable song strengths/flaws and development history.
- Make producers, writers, engineers and featured artists meaningfully alter process/outcomes.
- Expand genre-specific production choices without hard-locking creativity by genre.
- Connect song identity to reviews, fan segments, awards, sync, touring and long-tail catalog behavior.
- Keep title generation broad/non-repetitive and the compact wheel interaction.

## Protected decisions
- Writing, Recording, Producing, Charisma and Marketability remain independent core stats.
- Stage Presence, Touring and Viral Ability remain specialties rather than replacing core stats.
- A creative decision can improve commercial upside without guaranteeing a hit.
- Finished projects preserve the skill/development context used to make them.

## Do not drift
Do not create another parallel song-start form. Extend or consolidate the active Studio owner.
