# ENCORE — Finance / Economy / Wealth

## Audit score
**Finance core: 8.7/10** · **Wealth/ownership: 7.3/10**

## Current state
Finance uses exact-cent wallet storage, broad named income categories, action expenses, label shares, agent commission, career overhead, progressive fictional tax reserves and annual reconciliation. Weekly recaps expose revenue, costs, effective/marginal rates and spendable cash. Property, market holdings, catalogs and sports ownership provide secondary wealth gameplay.

## Canonical goal
Every dollar in ENCORE flows through one explainable ledger. Wealth systems create strategy, risk, leverage and legacy without overtaking the music-career game.

- Improve cash-flow forecasts for touring, releases and business commitments.
- Distinguish general career overhead clearly from named staff/agent costs.
- Make assets affect mobility, privacy, leverage, touring and business decisions.
