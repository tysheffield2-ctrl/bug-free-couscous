# ENCORE — PULSE / Social / Public Life

## Audit score
**7.3/10**

## Current state
PULSE supports multiple post formats, captions, linked releases, saved views/likes/comments/shares, follower and true-fan conversion, replies, industry posts, label discovery/leverage, organic growth and tour-specific social threads. Generated engagement currently has a 25% floor and no upper cap; sandbox can override followers and engagement.

## Canonical goal
PULSE becomes ENCORE’s public-life layer, not only a follower generator: fan communities, media narrative, artist interactions, reputation, crises, cosigns and cultural moments should emerge from it.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Calibrate the 25% engagement floor / uncapped rates against career scale and exploit scenarios.
- Add sentiment and public narrative distinct from raw engagement.
- Add verification/status, fan communities and platform trends.
- Add public cosigns, feuds, collaborations and industry conversation.
- Add staff/social strategy and crisis-response choices.
- Make regional virality feed Living World markets.
- Improve post/reply variety and persistent conversation history.
- Connect PULSE to negotiations without letting it overpower music performance.

## Protected decisions
Followers remain separate from true fans. Engagement above 100% can represent multiple interactions by one viewer and must remain backed by stored interaction counts.

## Do not drift
PULSE should submit discovery/conversion events to the Audience Engine instead of privately becoming a second fan model.
