# ENCORE — Living World & Geography

## Audit score
**7.5/10**

## Current state
The player has a hometown/current city, regional audience attribution, awareness, loyalty, demand, growth, scene heat and graphical market map. Touring consumes regional demand. NPC artists have location identity, and owned property can provide touring utility.

## Canonical goal
Cities should feel like music ecosystems rather than coordinates: scenes, genres, venues, promoters, media, economics and artist networks evolve over time.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Give each city stronger scene identity, costs and opportunity profiles.
- Add local promoters, venues, press/media and festivals that persist.
- Let genre scenes rise/fall/migrate.
- Add city-specific world events and economic changes.
- Track meaningful city bonds/history for artists.
- Let relocation affect relationships, cost of living, discovery and career strategy.
- Expand geography beyond the current U.S.-centric route model when the domestic model is stable.
- Feed geography into AI artist decisions, labels, touring, PULSE virality and commercial opportunities.

## Protected decisions
The player’s hometown remains permanent historical identity even if current city changes.

## Do not drift
Do not add geography that is purely decorative. Every new regional variable should change at least one meaningful decision.
