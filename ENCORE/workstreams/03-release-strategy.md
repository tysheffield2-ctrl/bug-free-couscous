# ENCORE — Release Strategy / Hype / Marketing

## Audit score
**7.9/10**

## Current state
ENCORE now supports pre-release song hype, planned album eras, post-release hype, campaign spending, album rollouts and PULSE-linked discovery. Hype decays, is capped at 100, allows limited weekly actions and boosts attention without guaranteeing success.

## Main issue
Promotion currently comes from several historical layers: weekly boosts, campaign/rollout logic, PULSE song boosts and the newer hype model. The gameplay is useful, but the math should become one release-strategy contract rather than stacked multipliers scattered across modules.

## Canonical goal
A release calendar in which the player defines objective, audience, budget, timing and channels, then watches anticipation, saturation, conversion and post-release momentum evolve.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Consolidate hype, marketing actions, album rollout and social promotion into one strategy model.
- Add release calendar/scheduling and explicit rollout phases.
- Add press, playlists/editorial, creators/influencers, radio-equivalent, live appearances and regional targeting.
- Model oversaturation, diminishing returns and audience fatigue.
- Let campaigns target songs, albums, regions or audience segments.
- Tie strong/weak rollout execution to label confidence, PULSE conversation, chart longevity and catalog discovery.

## Do not drift
Marketing should change opportunity and reach—not directly guarantee chart rank or quality.
