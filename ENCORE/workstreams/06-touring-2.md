# ENCORE — Touring 2.0

## Audit score
**8.6/10**

## Current state
Touring 2.0 is one of ENCORE’s strongest systems. It uses a canonical city/route engine settled weekly, regional demand, venue/price/production/security choices, standard and premium ticket inventory, travel distance, lodging, openers, show reports, regional aftermath, catalog/PULSE buzz, opening-slot invitations and archived history.

## Canonical loop
**Regional demand → route / venue / price → sales → performance → satisfaction → local + PULSE buzz → streams/fans → momentum → future demand**

## Highest-priority backlog
- Dynamic onsale/presale pacing before show week.
- Promoters, venue relationships and booking leverage.
- Deeper opener negotiation, availability and package-tour decisions.
- Festivals and special live opportunities.
- International routing and travel/logistics depth.
- Tour crew, rehearsals, fatigue, injuries, postponements and richer incidents.
- Tour-specific merch and sponsorship integration.
- Production evolution across legs rather than one static choice.
- Better visual route map and tour analytics.
- Long-tour storage/archival strategy that preserves important history without bloating saves.

## Protected decisions
- Maximum two performed/scheduled shows per week under the current pacing model unless explicitly redesigned.
- Route economics must reconcile exact ticket inventory and finance ledger totals.
- City demand—not global fame alone—drives live performance opportunity.
- Completed tour history survives cancellation/migration.

## Do not drift
Do not build Touring 3.0 beside this. Extend the canonical v2 route engine.
