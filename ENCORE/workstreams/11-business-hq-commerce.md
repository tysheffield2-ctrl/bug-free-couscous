# ENCORE — Business HQ / Merch / Commercial

## Audit score
**8.0/10**

## Current state
Business HQ provides an executive overview, business asset value, executive skills, recent decision history and access to offers, rights/contracts, label operations, merchandise, creative network, sports ownership, catalog market and team. Merch and commercial systems already have meaningful pricing, inventory and deal behavior.

## Canonical goal
Business HQ becomes the **single player-facing front door** for all non-performance business operations while preserving the underlying specialized systems.

## Highest-priority backlog
- Consolidate business-facing navigation under HQ instead of exposing every sub-system as an equal top-level destination.
- Add an HQ inbox for endorsements, media, production/collaboration requests, ownership decisions and expiring deals.
- Make executive skills materially affect business outcomes with transparent limits.
- Add consolidated cash-flow/business performance dashboards without duplicating Finance.
- Tie merch demand to eras, tours, regional loyalty, brand heat and fan segments.
- Deepen player-owned label/roster operations after the core artist career is stable.
- Improve delegation/team systems so a larger empire creates management decisions rather than pure passive bonuses.

## Protected decisions
Business HQ organizes business systems; it should not replace the artist-career Home screen.

## Do not drift
Do not create duplicate finance, contract or audience models inside Business HQ. HQ is an orchestrator and presentation hub.
