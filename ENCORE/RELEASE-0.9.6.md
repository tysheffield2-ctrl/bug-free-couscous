# ENCORE 0.9.6 Beta 1

- Added persistent AI-industry generations so new artists enter the world over time instead of relying indefinitely on the original roster.
- Added durable career identity for AI artists: generation, debut week, career stage, peak audience, retirement state and comeback history.
- Added long-career aging behavior with veteran, rebuild, legacy-transition and legacy-career stages.
- Added retirement without deleting artist IDs, catalogs, relationships or history; retired artists stop making new releases but remain part of the world.
- Added comeback behavior that restores the same persistent artist identity instead of generating a replacement copy.
- Added a changing genre climate that influences which genres produce the next generation of artists.
- Reworked synthetic song-chart competition into a shrinking safety floor: anonymous background entries disappear as persistent artist releases become numerous enough to fill the Top 100.
- Applied the same persistent-depth rule to the album Top 50.
- Added an Industry Generations section to World Pulse showing active careers, legacy artists, recent debuts, generation number and current genre climate.
- Added regression coverage for generation growth, stable artist IDs, retirement/comeback continuity, chart-filler retirement, album-chart depth, save validation and the 8 MB save budget.

This is the first Charts & AI Industry 2.0 foundation pass. Deeper AI release strategy, touring, label behavior, collaborations, retirement storytelling and full chart-history integration remain separate follow-up work inside the same workstream.
