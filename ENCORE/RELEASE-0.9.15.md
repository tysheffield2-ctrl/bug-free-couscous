# ENCORE 0.9.15-beta.1 — Cohesion Core 1.0

- Signed roster artists now remain active business relationships after the signature, with trust, contract satisfaction, momentum, ownership tension, leverage and label-support perception.
- Owned-label decisions now have lasting consequences: executive check-ins, priority support, creative autonomy and performance pressure move the relationship instead of acting as isolated buttons.
- Successful or unhappy signed artists can formally request renegotiation, early release or a catalog/master buyback.
- Roster agreements are archived into Contract Vault with durable agreement IDs, allowing later requests and disputes to refer back to the actual deal.
- Catalog participation can survive the service term and continue producing label receipts until ownership is repurchased, creating a real distinction between contract term and master rights.
- Catalog buybacks can transfer the covered master participation back to the artist instead of merely ending a contract.
- Legal Affairs is now a Business HQ department with a persistent civil docket, court-style case numbers, linked agreements, disputed clauses, exposure, response deadlines, counsel and resolution history.
- Lawsuits can be contested, settled or ignored; defense costs, settlements, judgments and reputation effects write back into the career instead of disappearing in a popup.
- High-leverage roster disputes can escalate from negotiation into an agreement-linked lawsuit.
- Artist File now keeps a permanent Legal / Business Record with links to the docket and Contract Vault.
- Representation is now the only player-facing representation surface; the duplicate legacy Agent page is no longer appended beneath the modern team system.
- Fixed the Representation Relationships challenge-action sign error so challenging a representative creates friction rather than accidentally increasing the relationship.
- Added a durable career event ledger foundation for future contract, ownership, rivalry, lawsuit, settlement and major-decision history.
