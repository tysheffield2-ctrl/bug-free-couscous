# ENCORE 0.9.3 Beta 1

- Redesigned Home around a backstage career hero, cash/fans/energy summary, latest release and compact Inbox, Offers and Artist File links.
- Organized Studio choices into Story, Sound and Ambition with explicit descriptions of each creative bonus.
- Replaced the rotating record selector with vertical, scroll-snapping wheels: five visible rows, fading edges and a fixed center highlight inspired by the iOS picker.
- Added touch scrolling and keyboard arrows, Page Up/Down and Home/End selection.
- Expanded natural song and album title vocabulary; dual slash titles now occur about 1% of the time instead of 25%.
- Browse 80 title ideas per batch, shuffle another batch or keep typing a custom title.
- Preserved creative brief fields when editing or shuffling a song title. Existing releases and saves remain intact.
- Retained the 0.9.2 career/business systems and separate normal/sandbox modes.

Validation: production build and targeted Studio, career/world, sandbox/save, release-experience and wheel/title regression checks. Full suite and real-device Safari interaction were not run in this workspace.
