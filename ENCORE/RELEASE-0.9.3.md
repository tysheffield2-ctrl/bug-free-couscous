# ENCORE 0.9.3 Beta 2

- Added a graphical, tappable U.S. audience map with city pins, audience size, audience share, career streams, demand, growth, awareness, loyalty and market rankings.
- Rebuilt the Artist File opening around career identity, current career state, hometown/current city, tour demand, tier progress and major career highlights.
- Upgraded Stats with an overall skill profile, separate ability and next-level XP progress bars, and quick training actions directly on every core ability and specialty.
- Raised generated PULSE engagement to a 25% minimum while removing the upper cap; rates above 100% now represent multiple likes, comments or shares per viewer and remain backed by saved interaction counts.
- Reduced the five-row Studio wheel footprint while keeping scroll math, touch selection and keyboard navigation aligned to the visible rows.
- Redesigned Home around a backstage career hero, cash/fans/energy summary, latest release and compact Inbox, Offers and Artist File links.
- Organized Studio choices into Story, Sound and Ambition with explicit descriptions of each creative bonus.
- Expanded natural song and album title vocabulary; dual slash titles now occur about 1% of the time instead of 25%.
- Preserved creative brief fields when editing or shuffling a song title. Existing releases and saves remain intact.
- Retained the 0.9.2 career/business systems and separate normal/sandbox modes.

Validation: production build, the existing ENCORE regression suite, and targeted 0.9.3 Beta 2 checks for PULSE engagement, the audience map, Artist File, Stats and compact wheel behavior.
