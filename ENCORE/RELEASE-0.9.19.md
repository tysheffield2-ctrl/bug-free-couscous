# ENCORE 0.9.19-beta.1

- Rebuilt the old Executive Team screen into a dedicated Executive Office for company leadership.
- Separated artist-side Representation from company-side leadership so managers, booking agents, publicists, personal attorneys and CPAs no longer double as the corporate C-suite.
- Added Chief of Staff, President / COO, CFO, General Counsel, CMO, Chief Creative Officer / Head of A&R, Head of Touring & Live, President of Catalog & Rights and President of International roles.
- Added company-scale progression so advanced executive seats unlock as audience, owned businesses and company value grow.
- Added rotating executive candidate markets with skill, industry reputation, operating style, salary, target bonus, specialty and occasional equity requests.
- Added explicit authority levels: Hands-On, Recommend, Guardrails and Full Authority. Hiring never silently grants spending or operating authority.
- Added weekly executive payroll, annual performance bonuses, severance obligations and permanent leadership history.
- Added executive KPIs, performance grades, trust, strategic alignment, effective skill and one-on-one relationship actions.
- Added company strategies including Balanced, Maximum Growth, Profitability, Artist Development, Global Expansion, Catalog Ownership, Market Domination and Independence.
- Added reserve-floor and delegated-spend guardrails for company policy.
- Added recurring executive meetings with real spending/tradeoff decisions, temporary operating plans and Business HQ priority alerts.
- Added founder-ownership tracking for executive equity grants.
- Wired delegated CFO/COO leadership into operating efficiency, CMO/creative leadership into release reach and touring leadership into live cost/demand estimates.
- Renamed the Business HQ destinations to Artist Representation and Executive Office to make the organizational split clear.
