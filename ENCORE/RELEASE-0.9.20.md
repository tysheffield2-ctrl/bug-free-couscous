# ENCORE 0.9.20-beta.1

- Expanded Legal Affairs into a career-wide cause-based risk system instead of limiting lawsuits to owned-label roster disputes.
- Added fifteen legal matter families: copyright/sample, songwriting credit, producer royalty, tour/venue, live injury, employment/termination, merch/vendor, sponsorship/endorsement, trademark/name, defamation/publication, property/lease, label interference/poaching, tax/regulatory, sports ownership and business partnership disputes.
- Added a live Legal Risk Monitor that shows modeled weekly filing risk, top active exposure sources and how to reduce them.
- Filing pressure now scales from actual career activity such as catalog size, collaborators, touring history/incidents, merchandise volume, brand deals, PULSE activity, properties, roster size, entity complexity, sports ownership and business holdings.
- Sandbox careers receive faster legal-event pressure and a bounded first-matter guarantee once meaningful exposure exists, making the system easier to play-test without changing normal-career cadence.
- Integrated Executive Office General Counsel into litigation. General Counsel now reduces filing pressure, becomes lead counsel on new matters and gains additional prevention value under Recommend/Guardrails/Full Authority.
- Kept the personal attorney as a fallback when the company has no General Counsel.
- Added outside litigation counsel as a paid option for difficult matters.
- Added pretrial mediation as an alternative to immediate settlement or full defense.
- Preserved the existing four-week response deadline, default-judgment consequence, defense resolution model and permanent civil docket history.
- Added matter-specific cause details, categories, risk-at-filing scores and prevention guidance to every generated case file.
- Preserved existing artist contract disputes and upgraded them to use the same General Counsel/case-management architecture.
